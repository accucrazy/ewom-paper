11
CONCLUSION

A New Form Demanding Its Own Analytical Lens

Our findings refine eWOM theory by shifting attention from the source of the message to the communicative features within synthetic eWOM — offering new directions for both research and practice in AI-mediated marketing communication.

Authenticity in AI-generated content is shaped by originality, distinct persona, clear product evaluations, and perceived credibility — not by the identity of the communicator.

Fashion sector salience: Persona construction and analytical thinking are particularly effective — fostering deeper engagement and enabling more nuanced messaging.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

1

All 8 variables are positively correlated — synthetic communication is inherently interconnected.

2

Originality + Persona (r = .57) jointly shape perceived authenticity in synthetic eWOM.

3

Sector-level differences reflect market behavior, not model limitations — the model mirrors real engagement dynamics.

#Action for Brands

3 Fashion brands: refine AI personas and deepen analytical content to differentiate and build stronger consumer connections.
