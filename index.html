Plate 02 — Background

The rise of AI-generated eWOM.

eWOM is the #1 source of brand discovery in the US and top-three in the UK and Germany. A new form has emerged — brands leverage social listening and consumer sentiment to produce AI-generated eWOM with a pre-set persona.

A.Traditional eWOM

Organic consumer reviews, UGC, and opinion sharing among individuals.

B.AI-Generated eWOM — new

Persona-driven synthetic content backed by real consumer data — operating as both poster and commenter.

RQ¹
Research Question One
What are the specific characteristics of AI-generated eWOM when explored from existing eWOM attributes?
RQ²
Research Question Two
What variables have emerged in AI-generated eWOM across fashion and other sectors?
Conceptual Frame We draw on message volume, credibility, valence and extend with analytical thinking, authenticity, originality, product evaluation, and persona.

Key ConstructPersona Mechanics
Plate 03 — Key Construct

Defining "persona" in AI-generated eWOM.

Persona is defined not as a human identity, but as a coherent synthetic framework encompassing three dimensions —

i.
Specific Traits
Character voice, tone, personality.
ii.
Expertise Boundaries
Domain knowledge scope and limitations.
iii.
Behavioral Patterns
Consistent response style and engagement logic.

Shanahan et al., 2023; Wan et al., 2025.

A worked example — excerpted from the dataset

Beauty & skincare category · mandelic-acid product · brand name anonymized.

Step 01 — Contextual Priming
Product positioning — a mandelic-acid skincare line framed within a "skincare minimalism after years of over-consumption" narrative.
Step 02 — Domain Expertise Injection
Mandelic acid · AHA / BHA / retinol distinctions · sebum & comedone regulation · "shelf-edit" framing.
Step 03 — Role-Playing via Dialogue Prompt
Confessional Dcard tone · late-20s skincare convert · journey-arc voice · sparing emoji.
Output — Distinct Digital Narrative · Brand → Consumer
"#Share — after years of skincare, the only things that stayed were mandelic acid + moisturizer. I used to think expensive = effective. A few years on, I keep repurchasing only three: a hydrating serum, a mandelic acid, a moisturizer — full circle to basics."

Plate 04 — Conceptual Model

Eight variables of AI-generated eWOM.

I.From Established eWOM Literature

01
Message Volume
Amount of brand-related information; visibility and cognitive-processing potential.
02
Credibility
Perceived trustworthiness, competence, and honesty of the source.
03
Valence
Emotional attitude — positive, negative, or mixed — toward the brand.

Mehrabian & Russell, 1974; Petty & Cacioppo, 1986; Hung et al., 2023.

II.Extended for AI-Generated eWOM

04
Analytical Thinking
Logicality, rigor, and hierarchical organization in expression.
05
Authenticity
"Sense of personal real experience" conveyed by the text.
06
Originality
Unique, non-repetitive, non-templated content.
07
Product Evaluation
Specific assessment of quality, design, and cost-effectiveness.
08
Persona
Coherent synthetic identity — traits, expertise, behavioral patterns.

Pennebaker et al., 2014; Boardman et al., 2022; Shanahan et al., 2023.

Plate 08 — Technical Approach

Supervised & reinforcement fine-tuning.

Our AI-generated eWOM was produced through a rigorous SFT pipeline with reinforcement learning — not simple prompt engineering.

01
Proprietary Data
Human-verified eWOM database from internal marketing operations — eliminating organic noise.
02

Non-verified insights are internalized rather than directly quoted — ensuring original synthesis.
03
Generalization
Cross-brand and cross-category adaptability while maintaining persona consistency.
04

Latent writing strategies and tactical angles encoded as training signals — learning implicit persuasion patterns.

Proprietary TechniqueCoT Training
Plate 09 — Proprietary Technique

Chain-of-Thought model training.

Our proprietary CoT training structures the generation process around three pillars — the model reasons through each before producing output.

i.

Product Info

Structured product attributes, pricing, positioning, and competitive context.

ii.

Author's Persona

Pre-defined traits, tone, expertise level, and behavioral consistency.

iii.

Storyline

The strategic bridge between brand promotion and natural consumer reception.

Why this matters The model learns to reason through these three pillars before generating — not merely pattern-match on surface text.

ConclusionKey Takeaways
Plate 12 — Conclusion

A new form demanding its own analytical lens.

Our findings refine eWOM theory by shifting attention from the source of the message to the communicative features within synthetic eWOM — offering new directions for both research and practice in AI-mediated marketing communication.

Authenticity Formation Authenticity in AI-generated content is shaped by originality, distinct persona, clear product evaluations, and perceived credibility — not by the identity of the communicator.
Fashion Sector Salience Persona construction and analytical thinking are particularly effective — fostering deeper engagement and enabling more nuanced messaging.

Key Takeaways

i.
All eight variables are positively correlated — synthetic communication is inherently interconnected.
ii.
Originality + Persona (r = .57) jointly shape perceived authenticity in synthetic eWOM.
iii.
Sector-level differences reflect market behavior, not model limitations — the model mirrors real engagement dynamics.

ImplicationsTheory & Practice
Plate 13 — Implications

Implications for theory & practice.

Theoretical Implications

№Contribution
i. Authenticity is shaped by message qualities, not the communicator's identity — extending our understanding of authenticity formation in synthetic eWOM.
ii. Originality & Persona are interdependent — working together to shape how authentic AI-generated content appears to audiences.
iii. Persona & Analytical Thinking contribute to theories of AI-mediated narrative, especially in fashion eWOM.

Practical Implications

№Action for Brands
i. Prioritize the four key drivers — originality, product evaluation, valence, credibility — to craft eWOM that resonates as genuine.
ii. Develop distinctive personas with clear product evaluations and detailed information for effective consumer evaluation.
iii. Fashion brands — refine AI personas and deepen analytical content to differentiate and build stronger consumer connections.
